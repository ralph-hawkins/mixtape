A test rig, not part of the trail. It answers one question: can a phone play three separate recordings at once and keep them perfectly in time for as long as they loop? Nothing here reads trail.js and nothing here can affect the walker's page.

How to read the result. Point all three layers at the same file (see the top of the code below) and turn them all on. Three copies of one recording, perfectly locked, sound like one recording. If they have drifted apart by even a few thousandths of a second you will hear it as an echo, a flange, or a hollow phasing sound. That is the whole test.
